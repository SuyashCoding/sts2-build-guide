# Silent — Poison Overview

Poison Silent revolves around the player dealing a significant amount of Damage over time, ramping up as you play more poison cards that chip away at the enemy's health. The amount of Damage done to an enemy is equivalent to the amount of poison stacked on them, which typically decreases by 1 at the end of each turn, seen as Decay. Poison bypasses Block when dealing Damage to an enemy's health.

- **#1 Rule: Poison bypasses Block**, make sure to utilize this to your advantage.
- **Poison Priorities:** Use cards that initiate Poison first, then cards that add to your Poison value, and then cards that multiply your Poison value. Make sure to intersperse Block cards in between those Poison cards to keep yourself alive.
- **Best Offense is a Good Defense:** Since chip damage is your main form of attack, defensive cards to block incoming attacks helps keep you alive while your damage-over-time strategy takes down your opponent.
- **Poison Power:** Make sure to keep using low cost cards to keep your opponent poisoned so their health steadily drains over the course of the battle.
- **Maximizing Multipliers:** Once poison is applied and you have a safe position, utilize cards that enhance or multiply the amount of poison damage to end the fight quicker.

## What to Aim For

The most important thing in your run is to stack as much Poison as possible on your enemies while you build up your Block counter to protect yourself as the chip damage wins you the match. Upgrading your Poison cards and especially the ones that multiply Poison damage are a must, **[Accelerant](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Accelerant)** which helps the Poison counter build up on enemies instead of staying the same when upgraded. Remember, Initiate Poison, Add Poison damage, and Multiply Poison!

## Key Relics

The following is a list of relics to keep an eye out for to help you progress and spike your deck's damage. **Specimen** is a very important relic to keep an eye out for clearing out hordes of enemies faster and more efficiently.

- **Ring of the Snake** - Draw 2 additional cards at the start of combat.
- **Snecko Skull** - When Poison is applied, apply an additional value of Poison damage.
- **Unsettling Lamp** - Doubles the first debuff effect applied to an enemy, letting you get twice the first Poison damage dealt.
- **Twisted Funnel** - Applies 4 Poison to all enemies at the start of combat.
- **Specimen** - Upon an enemy's death, transfer all remaining Poison values to a random enemy, ensuring no damage is wasted.

## Overall Attack

When playing a Poison Silent Build, your attack boils down to inflict Poison, build Block, Apply Debuff, and repeat for your gameplay loop, so boss breakdowns are going to look into whether you need to Stall your enemy, Race to kill them before they crush you, if they have a Setup Window for you to stack Poison, or if they have a straight up Poison weakness and are fair game.

## Act 1

For Act 1, your main priority should be building up your Poison and Defense cards for the rest of the game. It is vital to focus on grabbing a couple of cards that inflict Poison first as that will be your main form of attack, then cards that capitalize upon adding/multiplying the Poison value you inflicted on your enemies, and then grabbing some defense cards to keep you alive. Try to cycle through grabbing these cards so you have a nice healthy mix for your playthrough. Once you have a decent deck, try taking on paths that lead you to Uncommon and Rare cards so you have a chance to start looking for your heavy-hitting damage cards for later in the run.

### Overgrowth Elites

**Byrdonis** - Their strength increases over time, so scale up Poison to what you think is a good level and focus on Gaining Block as it takes them down, as it shouldn't be too hard a fight.

**Phrog Parasite** - Make sure to discard _Infection_ as Phrog will try to fill your deck with it. You'll have two free turns to deal with the Wrigglers that spawn after Phrog dies to Poison, so make sure to use those free turns wisely.

**Bygone Effigy** - You have two free turns to start the fight with, try to use them to initiate and add Poison, as Bygone Effigy will then deal 25 damage on each following turn. **[Catalyst](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Catalyst)** would be really effective to multiply Poison near the start of the fight, but just make sure to focus on stacking Poison as much as you can to take them down. Prioritize Poison over Block and you should make it through.

### Overgrowth Bosses

**Ceremonial Beast** - Your first turn with Ceremonial Beast is free, so use it to initiate Poison. It will proceed to attack and build its strength on most following turns, until it hits the Plow value, where it will spend one turn stunned and have its damage reset. It will then apply Ringing after being stunned, which will only let you play one card at a time. Try playing cards that let you Gain Block during the ringing turns, which occurs once every three turns, to keep yourself alive while the Poison does chip damage.

**Kin Priest** - Killing the Kin Priest will get rid of his followers, and with Poison being pretty target based, if you have a pretty solid way to scale Poison it's a priority to focus on attacking the Kin Priest specifically to win the fight. However, if you are still having trouble ramping up your damage quickly, it may be worth it to focus on eliminating one follower first in order to mitigate oncoming damage, but you should be clear to focus on the Kin Priest usually.

**Vantom** - Poison damage bypasses Vantom's Slippery gimmick, so focus on building up a steady stream of Poison and Block and this fight shouldn't be that rough.

### Underdocks Elites

**Shulking Colony** - AOE Poison cards are necessary for this fight as you're dealing with multiple enemies, and if you can build up enough Poison value early on, you should be able to coast to the finish with Block cards keeping you alive and healthy.

**Phantasmal Gardener** - Phantasmal Gardener gains a huge Block boost whenever they're hit, but lucky for you, Poison bypasses Block so you are in for a much easier time. Try to focus on taking them down one at a time, and try to use cards that Apply high amounts of Poison instead of multiple smaller cards.

**Terror Eel** - With the most health as an Act 1 elite, Terror Eel seems pretty scary, but with Plow activating at half health and letting you get in a free turn to Poison or Gain Block, you should be making pretty quick work of this heavy hitter. Be careful though, as it does pretty heavy damage and will apply Vulnerability on you during the fight, resulting in you taking 1.5 times the usual damage.

### Underdocks Bosses

**Waterfall Giant** - Waterfall Giant will try to stall you while building up for Steam Eruption to down you in one blow. Lucky for you, his constant applications of Weak do not lessen the impact of your Poison damage, so it's a race against time as you build your Poison damage to take the Giant down before Steam Eruption has enough power to send you into an early grave.

**Soul Fysh** - Soul Fysh takes turns 4 and 5 to treat itself to a break from most damage by becoming Intangible, so stacking as much Poison as possible before these turns is a must. Use the Intangible turns to Gain Block to protect yourself.

**Lagavulin Matriarch** - Poison is effective in bypassing Lagavulin Matriarch's Plating which is pretty effective for the fight. Be warned however, as if the Poison deals damage during the three turn grace period at the start of the fight, it will wake the Matriarch early. Focus on building up Block for those turns before you start ramping up the Poison to take the Lagavulin Matriarch out of the equation.

## Act 2

You should have a pretty decent hand and a couple of artifacts at this point. This should be when you get the final missing pieces for your build and sweep the game. The difficulty does spike a fair bit, but remember that any Elites you battle may drop the Relic or Card that may boost you over the finish line. Unless you're doing pretty bad damage wise, most Common and some Uncommon cards will not be worth your time to keep collecting, but keep an eye out for any tools you think may give you a better hand.

### Act 2 Elites

**Infested Prism** - Not too much is notable about the Infested Prism outside of high health and attack, but you do get 1 energy back the first time you hit him in a turn.

**Decimillipede** - You have to kill all segments of Decimillipede within 2 turns of each other, so AOE Poison cards are a great way to go here. If you can't kill them with AOE Poison, it may be worth your time to crunch some numbers to determine how many turns it'll take for your active Poison values to eat through the active segments so you can try timing them up to die at the same time.

**Entomancer** - Entomancer likes to add Dazed to your hand, which punishes dealing multiple small amounts of damage. Unfortunately for this boss, Poison really eats through their strategy, so alternating Poison and Block to keep yourself alive makes this boss pretty much a non-issue.

**Hunter Killer** - Hunter Killer likes to apply Tender, which lowers your Attack and Dexterity by 1 for every action you take, but Applying Poison and Gaining Block early on should make this fight pretty bearable.

**Kaiser Crab** - You take 1.5 times the damage from whichever enemy you are turned away from, being known as Crusher and Rocket, with Crusher occasionally applying Frail or Weak, while Rocket has a powerful attack with a cooldown. Upon either's death, the one remaining gains 99 Block and 5 Attack, though the block does little against your Poison damage.

**The Insatiable** - The Insatiable alternates between strength buildups and attacks, while its special Sandpit mechanic slowly ticks down. You can play one of six copies of _Frantic Escape_, given to you by the boss, to increase the counter at the cost of one energy per card.

**Knowledge Demon** - Knowledge Demon curses you on the 1st/5th/9th turn to either take a small set amount of damage per turn or deal with a limited amount of cards or energy. Taking a small set of damage is preferential, though make sure to keep your Block up.

## Act 3

If you've made it to Act 3, you're doing fine and should be just about ready to beat the game. This should be a piece of cake if all goes well, though picking up a Rare Card or 2 could always make it a bit easier. Good luck on the final stretch and let's hope that bad RNG doesn't mess up your run.

### Act 3 Elites

**Knights** - The trio of knights, consisting of Flail, Magi, and Ghost are a tough act. First priority should be Ghost, as by making your cards Ethereal, Ghost makes them disappear at the end of a turn if not played. Magi is up next in priority order as he hits like a truck and Dampen is not fun to play with as it downgrades all your cards. That just leaves Flail as the last one to take out.

**Mecha Knight** - Mecha Knight has Artifact, which discounts the first few debuffs, so play some of your cheaper cards to burn through the artifact before focusing on Applying Poison damage. Speaking of burn, he'll try to fill your hand with Burn, try to discard these before they burn you at the end of your turn.

**Soul Nexus** - Soul Nexus is just a straight up heavy hitter, so focus on building up Poison first, and then focus your energy on Gaining Block to live long enough to see the end of this fight.

### Act 3 Bosses

**The Doormaker** - The Doormaker consists of himself and his opener, the door. While the door is a heavy hitter, it does not have that much health. When it goes down, focus on putting as much Poison as you can on the Doormaker. When he portals away, the Poison will keep ticking down on him, making this a much easier fight.

**Test Subject** - Test Subject has three phases, gaining strength every time you play a skill in the 1st, shuffling 2 Wounds into your deck for each instance of unblocked damage in the 2nd, and going Intangible every other turn in the 3rd. Constant damage is the key. Focus on high damage Poison cards in the 1st phase, Gaining Block in the 2nd phase, and time big damage Poison spikes for his tangible appearances in the 3rd.

**Queen** - Queen is a pretty serious boss, though one with not too much health and no way to get rid of Poison. Her Chains of Binding, which makes it so that you can only play one of the first three cards drawn afflicted with the curse in a turn, is a hassle, but luckily they become unbound after the turn ends. As long as you aggressively target with Poison and ramp it up before switching over to Gaining Block to keep yourself alive, you should be clear for the win.
